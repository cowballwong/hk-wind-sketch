HK Wind Sketch · 香港風環境草圖 · v2

See how wind moves between buildings at street level

Search any place in Hong Kong, or click the map. Your browser fetches the government's building footprints for a 600 m square there and runs a small wind simulation on a flat slice about 2 m above the ground. Pick a wind direction, draw a new block, and look at it in plan or in 3D.

搜尋香港任何地方，或者喺地圖撳一下。你部電腦會攞嗰度 600 米見方嘅政府建築物資料，即時計一個離地大約 2 米嘅風場切面。揀風向、加座新樓，可以睇平面或者 3D。

A qualitative 2D sketch for teaching and early design feel. Not an Air Ventilation Assessment (AVA) under HPLB/ETWB TC No. 1/06: no wind velocity ratio (VR) figures, no pass or fail. The 3D view shows the same 2D slice among the real building heights; it is not a 3D simulation. 只係教學同早期設計參考嘅二維草圖，唔係 AVA，冇 VR 數字，冇合格不合格。3D 只係將同一個二維切面放喺真實樓高之間顯示，唔係三維模擬。

    How to read it 點樣睇

    • Wind corridors 風道: long yellow or orange bands along streets that line up with the wind.
    • Calm zones 靜風區: dark blue patches behind blocks (the wake) and inside courtyards.
    • Corner acceleration 轉角加速: orange or red spots where the wind squeezes round a corner.
    • White streaks follow the air. Eddies keep moving after the pattern settles; real wind is unsteady too.
    • Compare, don't measure: change one thing (a block, a gap, a direction) and look at what changes.

    What height does here 高度有咩用

    The simulation is one flat slice about 2 m above the ground. A block either stands in that slice or it doesn't, so for the wind, height decides only one thing: a block at least 2.5 m tall from its own base blocks the slice. A 10-storey and a 60-storey tower look the same to this sketch.

    The 3D view draws every block at its real height (LandsD top minus base level) so you can see the street section, but the coloured layer is still the 2D slice. Ground is flattened: blocks on a slope all stand on one level. In real streets tall towers also drag faster air down to the pavement (downwash), and wind comes over roofs as well as round the sides. A 2D slice shows neither. 3D 視圖用真實樓高，但風場仍然係二維切面；地形攤平咗；睇唔到高樓將高空風帶落地面。

    What this is not 呢個唔係乜

    • Not an AVA and not the TC No. 1/06 method, which needs 3D, wind directions weighted by how often they blow, VR at test points, and usually a wind tunnel for a Detailed Study.
    • No wind speeds, no VR, no comfort or safety rating, no pass or fail.
    • Nothing about wind loads on buildings (that is the Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019, for structural engineers).
    • 2D only: no terrain, trees, footbridges or covered walkways; open ground floors are not known; cells are about 2 m; the incoming wind is uniform and made up.

    How it works 原理

    The 600 m square is split into a grid of cells (300 x 300 at Normal). Each cell holds nine numbers: how much air is moving in each of nine directions. Every step the air hops to the next cell and the nine numbers relax towards a balanced mix; air that hits a wall bounces back. This is the lattice Boltzmann method (D2Q9, with a Smagorinsky turbulence model). Thousands of steps later, the street pattern appears. It runs on your own computer. When you pick a site, your browser asks the government's open-data services directly for the buildings and the wind table; nothing you draw is uploaded.
