HK Wind Sketch · 香港風環境草圖 · v3 preview

See how wind moves over and around buildings, in 3D

Search any place in Hong Kong, or click the map. Your browser fetches the government's buildings and ground levels for a 600 m square there and runs a small 3D wind simulation on your graphics card: air comes in from the direction you pick, climbs the slopes, goes over the roofs and squeezes round the towers. Draw your own building outline and see what changes, in plan or in 3D.

A qualitative teaching sketch on a coarse grid (cells about 4 to 6 m), for early design feel. Not an Air Ventilation Assessment (AVA) under HPLB/ETWB TC No. 1/06: no wind velocity ratio (VR) figures, no pass or fail. The 3D speeds are approximate: the grid is coarse, the incoming wind profile is assumed, and the model has not been checked against a wind tunnel. 只係教學同早期設計參考嘅草圖，格網粗（每格約 4 至 6 米），唔係 AVA，冇 VR 數字，冇合格不合格；三維風速只係大概。

    How to read it 點樣睇

    • Wind corridors 風道: long dark-blue bands along streets that line up with the wind.
    • Calm zones 靜風區: pale patches behind blocks (the wake), in courtyards and in hollows.
    • Corner acceleration 轉角加速: the darkest spots, where the wind squeezes round a corner or comes down the face of a tall tower.
    • Over and around 越過同繞過: in 3D, follow a streamline: low ones bend round the towers, higher ones lift over the roofs and drop behind them.
    • Compare, don't measure: change one thing (a building, a gap, a direction) and look at what changes.

    Ground and height 地形同高度

    The ground is LandsD's 5 m terrain model, so slopes such as the Mid-Levels are real, and the wind has to climb them. Each building stands on its own LandsD base level and rises to its LandsD top level, so towers on a slope keep their true roof heights. In the 3D model the air goes over roofs as well as round the sides, and tall towers can drag faster air down to the street.

    The solid shapes are kept simple: each ground spot is solid from the terrain up to the tallest roof standing on it, so open ground floors, footbridges, covered walkways and trees are not there. The terrain model itself includes elevated roads and tree canopy, which become solid ground here. 地形用地政總署 5 米地形模型；每座樓企喺自己嘅地面水平，樓頂高度係真；高架路同樹冠喺地形模型入面，會當實心。

    What this is not 呢個唔係乜

    • Not an AVA and not the TC No. 1/06 method, which needs wind directions weighted by how often they blow, VR at agreed test points, and usually a wind tunnel for a Detailed Study.
    • No wind speeds, no VR, no comfort or safety rating, no pass or fail. The 3D speeds are approximate.
    • Nothing about wind loads on buildings (that is the Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019, for structural engineers).
    • Coarse: cells are about 4.7 m at Normal, so narrow lanes and small gaps close up; the incoming wind profile is assumed; the edges of the square are pulled towards the incoming wind; the lid of the model is about 250 to 300 m up, and anything taller is cut off there.

    How it works 原理

    The 600 m square and the air above it are split into a 3D grid of cells (128 x 128 across at Normal, and as many layers as the buildings need). Each cell holds nineteen numbers: how much air is moving in each of nineteen directions. Every step the air hops to the next cell and the numbers relax towards a balanced mix; air that hits the ground or a wall bounces back; the top is a slippery lid. This is the lattice Boltzmann method (D3Q19, regularised BGK with a Smagorinsky turbulence model), run as WebGPU compute shaders, or as WebGL2 shaders where WebGPU is missing or would wake a laptop's big graphics card. The wind enters from your direction with a speed that grows with height (a power law reaching the reference speed at PlanD's 200 m level). Where the graphics card cannot do 3D, the page runs the v2 sketch instead, one flat slice about 2 m up, and says so under the picture. Nothing you draw is uploaded.
