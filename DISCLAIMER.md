# Disclaimer

HK Wind Sketch is a qualitative teaching and early-design sketch.

- It is **not** an Air Ventilation Assessment (AVA) and does not follow the method in HPLB/ETWB Technical Circular No. 1/06 and its Annex A Technical Guide (3D modelling, frequency-weighted wind directions, wind velocity ratio at test points, wind tunnel for a Detailed Study).
- It gives **no** wind speeds, wind velocity ratios, comfort or safety ratings, or pass or fail results, and must not be submitted to, or relied on for, any planning, building or lease application.
- The 3D view draws the same 2D slice among the buildings at their recorded heights on flattened ground. It is not a 3D simulation.
- It says **nothing** about wind loads or wind effects on structures (Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019).
