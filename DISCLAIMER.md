# Disclaimer

HK Wind Sketch is a qualitative teaching and early-design sketch on a coarse grid.

- It is **not** an Air Ventilation Assessment (AVA) and does not follow the method in HPLB/ETWB Technical Circular No. 1/06 and its Annex A Technical Guide (frequency-weighted wind directions, wind velocity ratio at agreed test points, wind tunnel for a Detailed Study).
- It gives **no** wind speeds, wind velocity ratios, comfort or safety ratings, or pass or fail results, and must not be submitted to, or relied on for, any planning, building or lease application.
- The 3D wind is a small lattice Boltzmann simulation with cells of about 4 to 6 m, simplified solids and an assumed incoming wind profile. Its speeds are approximate and it has not been checked against a wind tunnel. On devices without the graphics support it needs, the page shows a 2D slice instead and says so.
- It says **nothing** about wind loads or wind effects on structures (Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019).
- The building, terrain and wind data come from the Lands Department and the Planning Department of the HKSAR Government under the DATA.GOV.HK Terms and Conditions. The Government gives no warranty for the data, and users of the data indemnify the Government as those terms require.
- The software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Not official. Not professional advice.
